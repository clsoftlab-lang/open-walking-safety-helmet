Connect your helmet

Turn on the helmet and keep it close to this phone. Then choose “Connect helmet” and pick the device whose name starts with OWSH.

ⓘ The helmet assists your white cane or guide dog. It never replaces them.

⚠ Bluetooth is not available in this browser

Pairing with the helmet

The helmet only accepts phones that are paired with it. The first time you connect, your phone shows a pairing request. Accept it; no code is needed.

A new phone can pair while no phone is paired with the helmet, or within 2 minutes after the helmet is turned on.

If connection is refused, restart the helmet and connect within 2 minutes.

The demo helmet simulates alerts, status and an emergency so you can learn the app without hardware.

Connection tips
  • Turn Bluetooth on. Android may also need Location turned on to find nearby devices.
  • Only one phone can be connected to the helmet at a time.
  • If the connection drops, the app keeps trying to reconnect while it is open.
  • Keep this app open and the screen on. Phones pause web apps when the screen turns off.
